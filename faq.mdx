Answers for brands running Try with Mirra. Brand-specific policy (trial length, fees, exchanges) is what you set in [Try settings](/merchants/settings) and [return policies](/merchants/returns). Shoppers: [FAQ](/shoppers/faq).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When does the trial window start?">
    The trial starts when the order is **delivered**, not at checkout. Shoppers see **Trial ends in** in the [customer portal](/shoppers/customer-portal).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if the shopper does nothing?">
    Mirra reminds them before the due date. If they still do not complete the order, remaining items are treated as **kept** and charged.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When is the shopper charged?">
    They pay for kept items when they finish in the portal, or when the trial ends. Checkout may show a hold, a deposit, or \$0 — depending on how the store is set up.

    On a hold, the portal may mark amounts **Eligible for Instant Release**: return the item and the hold is released straight away. A deposit is refunded for items they return; kept items are charged for the remaining balance.

    A **Pending** or **Processing** line on the card is the hold, not a completed payment. Banks often keep it for 5–7 business days. Staff cannot remove it — only the bank can. A second pending line is usually a refreshed hold, not a second charge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can shoppers use Afterpay, a gift card, or a voucher at checkout?">
    No. Try checkout needs a saved card. PayPal, Afterpay, and other buy-now-pay-later options are not supported. A gift card or voucher can be applied later in the [customer portal](/shoppers/customer-portal) when they pay for kept items. See [Discounts](/merchants/promotions).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When is shipping charged?">
    If every line is try, shipping is usually charged at the end of the trial with kept items. Your Shopify free-shipping threshold still applies to those carts. If the cart also has a purchase, a gift, or shipping protection, shipping is charged at checkout. Shoppers still pay shipping if they return every try item. Click & Collect does not remove that outbound shipping charge. Details: [Storefront and checkout](/merchants/storefront).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a shopper get more time?">
    In the last 24 hours they may get a text and see **Request time extension** in the [customer portal](/shoppers/customer-portal), which adds 48 hours. Staff can also change the due date with **Set Payment Due Date** on [Try Orders](/merchants/try-orders).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if the card hold fails at checkout?">
    The Shopify order goes **On hold** with the tag **Failed to authorize payment**. Do not fulfil it. Mirra emails the shopper and retries for about **12 hours**, then cancels the order if **Cancel order when authorization fails** is on. Details: [Try orders](/merchants/try-orders#authorization-and-fulfillment).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if the card hold expires before they finish?">
    Banks typically keep a hold for about **7 days**. Mirra refreshes it about **24 hours** before it expires. Shopify Plus stores can have a longer Shopify hold. If capture still fails, Mirra retries and contacts the shopper. Do not fulfil **Expired** or **Failed to authorize payment** orders.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I refund after they have already been charged?">
    Yes. If the try order is settled and you later agree a refund, process it on the Shopify order. Store credit and gift card payouts still go through the [Returns Dashboard](/merchants/returns).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do shoppers use store credit or a gift card?">
    After you issue it, the portal shows **How to use your store credit** or **How to use your gift card**. Credit applies at checkout when they are logged in to that brand’s customer account. Gift cards have a code to enter at checkout. Until then they see **Your store credit isn't ready yet** (or the gift card equivalent).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can shoppers return purchased (non-try) items in Mirra?">
    Yes, when you use the Returns Dashboard for purchased returns. Payouts follow [return policies](/merchants/returns): refund, store credit, gift card, or exchange. If the dashboard shows **Setup mode — purchased returns are not live for customers**, shoppers cannot start those returns yet — trial returns still work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I offer styling sessions without try?">
    Concierge can be on for a shop even when you are still rolling out try. Keep **Visible to customers** off until you are ready. Add **Stores** for in-store sessions. New appointment types use **Virtual** (shopper picks video or phone) or **In-store**. Optional **Call & WhatsApp** is a tap-to-contact row, not a booking. Shoppers book from the product page or portal — they **Choose a store** when more than one location is available. See [Concierge bookings](/merchants/concierge).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do shoppers open the customer portal from Shopify?">
    On the Shopify order status page they may see **Open Mirra Customer Portal**. They also get the same link in delivery and reminder messages. Staff can copy the URL from [Try Orders](/merchants/try-orders).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    In the Mirra app open **Support** — **Email**, **Call**, Knowledge Base, and FAQs. Or [book a demo](https://meetings-ap1.hubspot.com/lahern/website-meeting-link?). Shoppers should contact **your brand** first. Portal login issues: [support@trywithmirra.com](mailto:support@trywithmirra.com).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
