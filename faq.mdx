Answers for shoppers and merchants. Brand-specific policy questions (trial length, fees, exchanges) go to the store the order came from.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When does the trial window start?">
    The trial starts when the order is **delivered**, not at checkout. Shoppers see **Trial ends in** in the [customer portal](/shoppers/customer-portal).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if the shopper does nothing?">
    Mirra reminds them before the due date. If they still do not complete the order, remaining items are treated as **kept** and charged.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When is the shopper charged?">
    They pay for kept items when they finish in the portal, or when the trial ends. Checkout may show a hold, a deposit, or \$0 — depending on how the store is set up.

    On a hold, the portal may mark amounts **Eligible for Instant Release**: return the item and the hold is released straight away. A deposit is refunded for items they return; kept items are charged for the remaining balance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do shoppers use store credit or a gift card?">
    After the store issues it, the portal shows **How to use your store credit** or **How to use your gift card**. Credit applies at checkout when they are logged in to that brand’s customer account. Gift cards have a code to enter at checkout. Until then they see **Your store credit isn't ready yet** (or the gift card equivalent).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can shoppers return purchased (non-try) items in Mirra?">
    Yes, when the brand uses the Returns Dashboard for purchased returns. Payouts follow [return policies](/merchants/returns): refund, store credit, gift card, or exchange.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I offer styling sessions without try?">
    Concierge can be on for a shop even when you are still rolling out try. Keep **Visible to customers** off until you are ready. Add **Stores** for in-store sessions. Shoppers book from the product page or portal — they **Choose a store** when more than one location is available. See [Concierge bookings](/merchants/concierge).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do shoppers open the customer portal from Shopify?">
    On the Shopify order status page they may see **Open Mirra Customer Portal**. They also get the same link in delivery and reminder messages. See [Customer portal](/shoppers/customer-portal).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Shoppers: contact the **brand** first. Portal login issues: [support@trywithmirra.com](mailto:support@trywithmirra.com).

    Merchants: in the Mirra app open **Support** — **Email**, **Call**, Knowledge Base, and FAQs. Or [book a demo](https://meetings-ap1.hubspot.com/lahern/website-meeting-link?).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
