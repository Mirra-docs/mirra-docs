Try with Mirra is a Shopify app for **try before you buy**. Shoppers order a trial, wear or use the items at home, then keep what they want and return the rest.

## For shoppers

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Place a try order" icon="bag-shopping" href="/shoppers/place-a-try-order">
    Add try items on the storefront, check out, and wait for delivery.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Customer portal" icon="user" href="/shoppers/customer-portal">
    Keep, return, or exchange items and pay for what you keep.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Send items back" icon="box-open" href="/shoppers/returns">
    Download a label, ship the return yourself, or drop items at a store.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
