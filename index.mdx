Try with Mirra is a Shopify app for **try before you buy**. Shoppers order a trial, wear or use the items at home, then keep what they want and return the rest.

This tab is for **merchants**. Shopper guides (portal, keep / return, sending items back) are on the **Shoppers** tab.
