## Convert more shoppers with Try at Home

Mirra helps fashion and apparel brands on Shopify convert more first-time shoppers by letting customers try before they buy. Shoppers select items at zero upfront cost, try them at home, and confirm keep or return through a branded portal. Alongside Try at Home, Mirra offers personal styling services, a unified returns platform, and soon, behavioral analytics. The result is higher conversion, larger average order value, and less reliance on discounting.

## Get up and running in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install on Shopify">
    Add Mirra from the Shopify App Store and connect your store in one click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your Try at Home experience">
    Set your trial window, item limits, and payment authorization rules in the Mirra dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live and track results">
    Publish the Try at Home button on your product pages, then monitor conversion, AOV lift, and return rates.
  </Step>
</Steps>
