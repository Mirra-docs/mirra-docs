**Marketing Setup** in the Mirra app is a launch checklist. Complete the steps as you publish homepage copy, a how-it-works page, and launch emails. Templates live in the [Help Center](https://helpcenter.trywithmirra.com/knowledge-base).

## Open the checklist

In the Mirra app, go to **Marketing Setup**. Mark each step done as you finish it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Marketing preparation">
    Click **Download Marketing Guidelines**. That opens the retailer marketing guide in the Help Center.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Website homepage">
    Add an **Announcement Bar**, update a **Homepage Banner**, and add a **How it Works Section**. **Add to Announcement Bar** and **View Banner Template** open Help Center copy. **Update Banner** and **Design your section** open the theme editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="How it works page">
    Click **Create Page** to open the theme editor. Use **View How it Works Template** for page copy you can paste.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Engaging customers">
    Plan an **Email Launch Campaign** and add try-before-you-buy to existing flows such as abandoned cart. Open **View Email Template** in the Help Center.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Social media marketing">
    Use the Help Center post, story, and ad templates. **Advertise** links open Meta ad layouts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Theme and button placement still live in **Onboarding** and [Storefront and checkout](/merchants/storefront). This page is brand messaging, not the try button itself.
</Tip>
