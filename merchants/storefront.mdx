Shoppers start a try order from your storefront. You enable that in the Shopify theme editor, then finish **Select Settings & Preferences** in **Onboarding**.

Short placement how-tos: [Customise your Mirra storefront](https://helpcenter.trywithmirra.com/knowledge-base/merchant-guides).

## Turn on the app embed

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Onboarding">
    In the Mirra app, go to **Onboarding** and complete **Turn On App Embed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Try with Mirra">
    In the theme editor, turn on the **Try with Mirra** app embed (it sits on the page body). This drives the how-it-works modal, cart banner, and add-to-cart behaviour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the product button">
    Complete **Add Button to Product Pages**. Add the **Try with Mirra Button** block on your product template.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until you click **Go Live**, changes stay in **Preview mode**.

## Theme blocks

| Theme editor name | Shoppers see |
| --- | --- |
| **Try with Mirra** (app embed) | How-it-works modal, cart try toggle, add-to-cart |
| **Try with Mirra Button** | Product-page try button |
| **TBYB button layout** | Layout wrapper around the product button |
| **Try Before You Buy Guide** | Shop / Try / Pay explainer |
| **Try with Mirra Cart Addon** | Extra cart messaging |
| **Mirra Concierge Booking** | **Book a styling session** on the product page (if concierge is on) |

On **Try with Mirra Button**, set **Button Text**, **Button Background** / **Button Text Color**, **Max Button Size**, **Min Button Height**, **Margin Top**, **Alignment**, and mobile width.

**Mirra Concierge Booking** has its own appearance settings: **Use Shop Font**, **Presentation** (**Centered modal** or **Slide-out panel**), **Header background**, **Panel background**, **Option layout** (**Cards** or **Divided list**), **Panel width**, **Action background**, **Action text**, **Control corner radius**, plus size and alignment. **Custom CSS** overrides those after you set them.

## Button and modal copy

The product button label is merchant-chosen. Defaults include **Try Before You Buy**, **Home Try-On**, **Try Now, Pay Later**, **Try at Home**, **Add to Bag**, and **Try Me First**.

The modal headline is **TRY BEFORE YOU BUY**. Shoppers step through selecting try, payment hold or deposit language, **Try at Home** (your trial days), and **Easy Returns**.

App embed settings you can change:

- **Use Shop font for info pop-up**
- **Info pop-up primary Color**
- **Replace cart selling plan text with Try with Mirra logo**
- **Open Cart After Adding to Cart**
- **Reload page after adding to cart**
- **Terms and Conditions URL**

<Note>
  Cart toggle copy: **Try Before You Buy all eligible items**. Shoppers can mix try and buy in one checkout when some lines are not try-eligible.
</Note>

## Checkout

Enable the Try with Mirra checkout blocks and functions in **Shopify Admin → Settings → Checkout**. When you add a Try with Mirra checkout customization, Mirra can turn the matching function on automatically.

On try checkouts, shoppers may see:

- **Click to Try Before You Buy** on a line
- A card-authorization checkbox: they must accept that the card will be authorised for the total shown
- Min/max try item errors from your **Try Item Limit**
- A phone number requirement if your store needs it for the portal

PayPal can be hidden on try carts. Custom try shipping rates, if you use them, are set in Shopify shipping — see [Getting started](https://helpcenter.trywithmirra.com/knowledge-base/getting-started) in the Help Center.

## Order status page

The **Customer Portal** app block can appear on the Shopify **order status** page (customer accounts). Shoppers see **Manage your trial or return in the Mirra Customer Portal. Purchased items follow our usual returns policy.** and **Open Mirra Customer Portal**.

The button opens that order’s customer portal. You can move or pin the block in the Shopify customer account editor. Shoppers still get portal links by email and SMS as well.
