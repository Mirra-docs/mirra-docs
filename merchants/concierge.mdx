Concierge lets shoppers **Book a styling session** from the product page and the customer portal. You manage bookings in the **Returns Dashboard**.

The Concierge section appears only if concierge is on for your shop. If you see **Concierge is not available**, contact [support@trywithmirra.com](mailto:support@trywithmirra.com).

Add the **Mirra Concierge Booking** theme block so the CTA shows on product pages. See [Storefront and checkout](/merchants/storefront).

## Bookings

Open **Bookings**. Tabs: **All** · **Pending** · **Confirmed** · **Contacted** · **Completed** · **Cancelled**. Sort and filter by date from the same header menu as the returns inbox.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review a request">
    Open a booking. **Approve booking** or **Reject**. You can also **Reschedule**, **Change time**, or **Confirm new time**. In-store bookings show the store the shopper chose. Answers to your pre-booking questions appear under **Booking answers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a booking">
    Click **New booking**, then **Create booking**, if staff are booking on behalf of a shopper. For in-store types, pick a store first. Pre-booking questions on that type show as **(optional)** — you can leave them blank.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Shoppers get email (and optional SMS) when a request is received, confirmed, reminded, cancelled, or updated. You can send the same moments through [Klaviyo](/merchants/klaviyo).

## Content

Open **Content**. Click **New content**. Filter **All**, **Films**, or **Tips**. Reorder the customer menu (appointment types plus films and tips).

Default menu label: **Book a styling session**. Headlines, subtext, and appointment names are yours to edit.

## Settings

Open **Settings** (under Concierge). Tabs: **Bookings** · **Availability** · **Communications** · **Integrations**.

Until you turn on **Visible to customers**, copy is **Setup only — customers cannot see or book Concierge yet.** Shoppers cannot book from the portal or product pages while you finish setup.

### Stores

On the **Bookings** tab, **Stores** are the locations shoppers can attend for **In-store** appointments.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a store">
    Click **Add store**. Set **Store name**, **Address line 1**, **City**, **Postcode**, and **Country** (pick the country by name). Optional: **Address line 2**, **State / province**, **Phone**, and **Timezone**. When **Phone** is set, in-store booking emails include it with the store name and address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set hours and notifications">
    Turn on **Available for bookings** so shoppers can choose this location. Use **Custom store hours** if this location should not inherit the default concierge hours.

    Under **Booking notifications**, set **Store notification email** and **Send notifications to**: **Main concierge inbox only**, **Store email only**, or **Both store and main inbox**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map in-store appointment types">
    Edit each **In-store** appointment type. Choose **All enabled stores** (new stores are included automatically) or **Selected stores only**. Add and enable a store before you offer an in-store type.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Disabled stores stay on historical bookings but are hidden from shoppers.

### Appointment types

Click **New type**. Set **Name**, **Duration** (**10 minutes** through **120 minutes**), and **About** — longer copy shoppers see under the name when they choose a type.

**Channel** is **Virtual** (shopper picks video or phone) or **In-store**. For **Virtual**, tick **Offered methods**: **Video call** and/or **Phone call**. Shoppers only choose a method when both are on.

Also set:

- **Available to customers**
- **Booking inbox email** — new booking alerts and customer replies for this type. Leave blank to use the shop-wide notification and reply-to emails (or the store email for in-store types)
- **Where customers can book**: **Customer portal**, **Product pages**, or both
- Approval: **Auto-confirm all bookings**, **Require approval for all bookings**, or **Auto-confirm during set hours**

Older types may still show **Video call** or **Phone call** as **(legacy)**. New types use **Virtual**.

### Pre-booking questions

On an appointment type, **Pre-booking questions** are what shoppers answer after they pick a time. Add only what you need before the appointment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a question">
    Click **Add question**. Choose **Short text**, **Long text**, **Date**, **Single choice**, or **Multiple choice**. Set **Question**, turn **Required** on or off, and use **Add description** if you want extra guidance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add choices">
    For **Single choice** or **Multiple choice**, fill in **Choices** and click **Add choice**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reuse another type">
    **Copy from another type** starts from questions you already wrote. If this type already has questions, confirm **Replace this type’s questions with a copy of the selected form?**
  </Step>
</Steps>

A **Customer preview** sits next to the editor. On a narrow screen, switch **Edit questions** and **Customer preview**. You can add up to 20 questions. Drag to reorder them.

Shoppers see these under **Your questions** in the portal and on the product-page booking panel. Each question is marked **required** or **optional**.

### Microsoft 365 and Outlook

On an appointment type, **Microsoft 365** connects the mailbox that should own the calendar events.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save the type first">
    Save the type, then click **Connect a new account**. Choose **No Microsoft account** or a connected mailbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an Outlook calendar">
    Set **Outlook calendar**. New bookings use that calendar. Existing synced bookings stay where they are.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Block busy time (optional)">
    Turn on **Block times that are busy in Outlook**. Events marked **Busy** or **Out of Office** hide those slots. **Tentative** events are ignored.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On **Integrations**, **Microsoft 365** copies confirmed bookings to Outlook and creates Teams meetings for video calls.

On a booking, **Details** can show **Join with Teams**. If sync failed you will see **This booking has not synced to Outlook**.

Confirmed video bookings can include a Teams join link. Shoppers see **Join the Video Call**, which unlocks 15 minutes before the start (**Available from** a time).

Times on the booking receipt and in concierge emails use the store timezone. After they book a video call, **How to join** tells them where the link is:

- **Your video link is in your email** — the confirmation email includes it
- **We'll email your video link before your appointment** — a reminder is scheduled, and that email includes the link
- **We'll email your video link shortly** — the link is not ready yet

If they cannot find the confirmation, the receipt says **Can't find your confirmation? Check your junk or spam folder.**

### Call and WhatsApp

**Call & WhatsApp** is optional contact on the Concierge list — shoppers tap to call or open WhatsApp. It is not a booking.

Click **Add Call or WhatsApp**, then **Add Call** or **Add WhatsApp**. Set the number and label. Blank labels show **Call us** or **WhatsApp us**. Use **Add a country override** / **Add country** if a ship-to country should use a different number.

### Concierge button

**Concierge button** sets the portal and product-page call-to-action. Click the title to edit **Button title** (default **Book a styling session**). **Add a button image** is optional — without one, shoppers see text only.

### Customer photos

On the **Bookings** tab, **Customer photos** controls the booking flow: **Don't ask**, **Optional**, or **Required**.

### Availability

Weekly hours, blocked time, holiday suggestions, and timezone. Store-level hours override this when **Custom store hours** is on.

### Communications

Merchant: **New booking**, **Customer amended booking**.

Customer: **Request received**, **Booking confirmed**, **Appointment reminder**, **Booking cancelled**, **Status updated**, **Booking rescheduled**.

SMS (when enabled): booking confirmed / cancelled, appointment reminder, status updated.

Set notification email and **Reply-to email**. In an email **Body**, **Click a token to insert it into the body**. `{{store_phone}}` is the store phone from **Add store**. Under **Email appearance**, click **Open Emails** to brand concierge and purchased-return mail — see [Emails](/merchants/emails). **From name** there applies to concierge emails as well as purchased returns. Try complete-order emails stay Mirra-branded.

### Customer visibility

On **Settings**, turn on **Visible to customers** when you are ready.
