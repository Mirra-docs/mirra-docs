Concierge lets shoppers **Book a styling session** from the product page and the customer portal. You manage bookings in the **Returns Dashboard**.

The Concierge section appears only if concierge is on for your shop. If you see **Concierge is not available**, contact [support@trywithmirra.com](mailto:support@trywithmirra.com).

Add the **Mirra Concierge Booking** theme block so the CTA shows on product pages. See [Storefront and checkout](/merchants/storefront).

## Bookings

Open **Bookings**. Tabs: **All** · **Pending** · **Confirmed** · **Contacted** · **Completed** · **Cancelled**. Sort and filter by date from the same header menu as the returns inbox.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review a request">
    Open a booking. **Approve booking** or **Reject**. You can also **Reschedule**, **Change time**, or **Confirm new time**. In-store bookings show the store the shopper chose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a booking">
    Click **New booking**, then **Create booking**, if staff are booking on behalf of a shopper. For in-store types, pick a store first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Shoppers get email (and optional SMS) when a request is received, confirmed, reminded, cancelled, or updated. You can send the same moments through [Klaviyo](/merchants/klaviyo).

## Content

Open **Content**. Click **New content**. Filter **All**, **Films**, or **Tips**. Reorder the customer menu (appointment types plus films and tips).

Default menu label: **Book a styling session**. Headlines, subtext, and appointment names are yours to edit.

## Settings

Open **Settings** (under Concierge). Tabs: **Bookings** · **Availability** · **Communications**.

Until you turn on **Visible to customers**, you will see **Hidden from customers**. Shoppers cannot book from the portal or product pages while you finish setup.

### Stores

On the **Bookings** tab, **Stores** are the locations shoppers can attend for **In-store** appointments.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a store">
    Click **Add store**. Set **Store name**, **Address line 1**, **City**, **Postcode**, and **Country** (pick the country by name). Optional: **Address line 2**, **State / province**, **Phone**, and **Timezone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set hours and notifications">
    Turn on **Available for bookings** so shoppers can choose this location. Use **Custom store hours** if this location should not inherit the default concierge hours.

    Under **Booking notifications**, set **Store notification email** and **Send notifications to**: **Main concierge inbox only**, **Store email only**, or **Both store and main inbox**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map in-store appointment types">
    Edit each **In-store** appointment type. Choose **All enabled stores** (new stores are included automatically) or **Selected stores only**. Add and enable a store before you offer an in-store type.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Disabled stores stay on historical bookings but are hidden from shoppers.

### Bookings

- Appointment types
- Duration: **10 minutes**, **15 minutes**, **30 minutes**, **45 minutes**, **60 minutes**, **90 minutes**, or **120 minutes**
- Channels: **Video call** · **Phone call** · **In-store**
- **Where customers can book**: **Customer portal**, **Product pages**, or both
- Approval: **Auto-confirm all bookings**, **Require approval for all bookings**, or **Auto-confirm during set hours**

### Availability

Weekly hours, blocked time, holiday suggestions, and timezone. Store-level hours override this when **Custom store hours** is on.

### Communications

Merchant: **New booking**, **Customer amended booking**.

Customer: **Request received**, **Booking confirmed**, **Appointment reminder**, **Booking cancelled**, **Status updated**, **Booking rescheduled**.

SMS (when enabled): booking confirmed / cancelled, appointment reminder, status updated.

Set notification email, reply-to, and email theme (logo, colours, footer).

### Customer visibility

On **Settings**, turn on **Visible to customers** when you are ready. While it is off, copy is **Setup only — customers cannot see or book Concierge yet.**
