Open **Integrations** in the Mirra app. Connect the tools you use for labels and extra Shopify access. [Klaviyo](/merchants/klaviyo) and [MCP](/merchants/mcp) have their own pages.

## Optional Shopify permissions

**Optional permissions** — click **Accept** if Shopify asks for extra scopes. You need these for store credit, gift cards, and exchanges. If a payout or exchange control is disabled, grant permissions here, then try again.

## Exchanges

**Exchanges V2** — **Enable Exchanges V2** / **Disable Exchanges V2**. When on, shoppers pick a replacement on your storefront during the [exchange window](/merchants/settings). Also turn on **Offer exchange on try orders** in **Settings → Returns**.

## Return label providers

Connect a provider before you select its method in [Return shipping](/merchants/return-shipping).

| Card | Merchant actions |
| --- | --- |
| **Australia Post** | **Turn on** / **Turn off** for eParcel return labels. Enter **Account Number**, **API Key**, and **API Secret**, then click **Test connection**. A 7-digit eParcel account is padded to 10 digits; an 8-digit StarTrack account is left as entered. Success shows **Australia Post connection succeeded**. **Using Mirra's eParcel** means labels are generated on Mirra’s account — no credentials needed on this card |
| **Starshipit** | API key, **Return Addresses**, and carrier (Australia Post, Aramex, UPS, FedEx, USPS, Royal Mail, DHL, and others listed in the form) |
| **Royal Mail** | API for UK return labels |
| **Shippo** | USA labels — **General Settings**, **Default Package Settings**, **Return Addresses**. Click **Check product weights** (samples up to 20 try products) before you enable. Return labels use Shopify variant weights. If products have no weight, add weights in Shopify and check again, or tick **I accept the risk that customers may have issues at the post office** to enable anyway. |

## Mirra Store

**Mirra Store** lists your try products in the Mirra mobile app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the setup guide">
    Click **View Setup Guide**. Configure collections, search, and filtering as that guide describes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept optional permissions">
    Complete **Optional permissions** on this page first. Opt-in stays disabled until those scopes are accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Click **Opt-In to Mirra Store**. Click **Opt-Out of Mirra Store** to leave later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Warehouse and ERP

These tools are not configured on this screen. Keep them in Shopify or the provider’s admin. Ship only Shopify orders that are **Authorized** and tagged **Try with Mirra**. Skip **Failed to authorize payment** / **On hold**. See [Try orders](/merchants/try-orders#authorization-and-fulfillment).

| System | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Cin7 Omni** | Import only **Authorised** and **Paid** Shopify orders. Send Mirra an API key, or invite [support@trywithmirra.com](mailto:support@trywithmirra.com) as a user. |
| **Apparel21** | Keep your existing Shopify connector for purchased orders and skip **Try with Mirra** tags. Mirra writes try orders into Apparel21 as a normal sale, then a refund if items come back. Fulfil them like other web orders. |

<HelpCenter>
  Click-by-click: [Cin7 Omni](https://helpcenter.trywithmirra.com/knowledge-base/setting-up-your-cin7-omni-integration) and [Apparel21](https://helpcenter.trywithmirra.com/knowledge-base/how-try-with-mirra-works-with-apparel21).
</HelpCenter>

## Other returns portals

Loop, ReturnGO, and similar tools can stay on for purchased returns. Do not auto-refund orders tagged **Try with Mirra** in those tools. Details: [Returns](/merchants/returns#other-returns-portals).

## Revoke AI access

If you have connected AI assistants, **MCP** appears here. **Revoke access** disconnects every AI client for the shop. Staff can reconnect later. Details: [Connect AI assistants](/merchants/mcp).
