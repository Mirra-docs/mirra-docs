Use **Discounts**, **Gift Rules**, and **Samples** in the Mirra app to attach offers to try orders. These are separate from Shopify discount codes you already run.

Help Center: [Discount & Promo Codes for Mirra Orders](https://helpcenter.trywithmirra.com/knowledge-base/merchant-guides).

## Discounts

Open **Discounts**. Click **Create Discount** (or **Edit Discount** on an existing one).

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Internal name for the discount |
| Savings | Amount as $ or % |
| **Is Automatically Applied** | Apply without a code when the cart qualifies |
| **Minimum Item Requirements** | How many try items are needed |

## Gift rules

Open **Gift Rules**. Click **Create gift rule**.

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Gift product | The free (or reduced) item added to the try order |
| **Minimum spend** | Order total that unlocks the gift |
| **Gift item cost** | How much the gift counts as if they keep it |

Shoppers see gift lines in the portal with a **Gift** pill. Portal copy explains that they may need to keep enough of the order to qualify.

## Free samples

Open **Samples**. Click **Add Rule**.

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Sample product | The sample variant |
| **Quantity** | How many of that sample |
| **Trigger** | **Global** (any try order) or specific products |

Checkout allows **one free sample item per order**, and only on try-before-you-buy orders.
