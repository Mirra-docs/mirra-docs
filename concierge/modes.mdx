Mirra Concierge gives you four flexible styling modes. You can activate one mode or any combination, and each mode is designed to serve a different type of customer and decision moment. Choose the mix that fits your brand, inventory, and customer service style.

## Concierge touchpoints

Regardless of which mode you use, Concierge can be surfaced in three places:

- **PDP**: a styling CTA appears next to the buy button to catch hesitation before checkout
- **Try at Home portal**: styling support is available while the customer is trying items at home and deciding what to keep
- **Returns portal**: a last-chance styling check-in appears before a return is finalised, often preventing unnecessary returns

## The four Concierge modes

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Virtual Styling Call">
    A live, one-to-one video or voice call between your customer and a trained stylist.

    **Best for:**

    - High-value orders where the customer needs confidence to proceed
    - First-time shoppers unsure about sizing across your collections
    - Customers who want real-time advice to build a full outfit

    **How it works:**

    1. The customer taps "Book a styling call" from the PDP, Try at Home portal, or Returns portal.
    2. They choose an available time slot.
    3. The stylist joins the call prepared with the customer's browsing and order context.

    **Key details:**

    - Calls are scheduled, not on-demand
    - Stylist notes are saved against the customer profile for future sessions
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="In-Boutique Appointment">
    A booked appointment at a physical store location, bridging online browsing with an in-person experience.

    **Best for:**

    - Customers who prefer to feel fabric and try items on in person
    - Brands with a flagship or store network
    - High-AOV transactions where personal attention drives conversion

    **How it works:**

    1. The customer books a time slot at the nearest boutique directly from the Concierge interface.
    2. Mirra detects the nearest store automatically.
    3. The appointment and any resulting purchase or keep decision are tracked back to the original Concierge booking for clean attribution.

    **Key details:**

    - Requires at least one boutique location configured in your Concierge settings
    - Online-to-offline revenue is attributed to the initial Concierge engagement
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Styling Video">
    A short, reusable video that demonstrates how to wear, style, or size a piece.

    **Best for:**

    - Popular items purchased frequently or included in Try at Home orders
    - Brands with strong visual identity on social media
    - Reducing stylist labour by creating a content asset once

    **How it works:**

    1. Your team (or Mirra's content support) produces a short video for a product or category.
    2. The video is attached to the relevant PDPs and portal experiences.
    3. Customers watch the video before buying, during their trial, or while considering a return.

    **Key details:**

    - A single video is reused across many orders and customers
    - Excellent for fit guidance and "how to style" tutorials
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Styling Notes">
    Tailored written guidance delivered alongside an order or trial box.

    **Best for:**

    - Brands with a lean team who cannot staff live calls
    - Standardised fit advice that personalises based on customer size or preferences
    - Complete-the-look suggestions that increase basket size

    **How it works:**

    1. You create note templates that include fit guidance, care instructions, and pairing suggestions.
    2. Mirra personalises the note based on the customer's order, size, and profile.
    3. The note appears in the Try at Home portal, Returns portal, or as an email depending on your setup.

    **Key details:**

    - Notes can include "complete the look" prompts to increase AOV
    - Templates are maintained in your Concierge settings and updated over time
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
