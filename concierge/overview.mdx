Mirra Concierge is a styling guidance layer that helps fashion brands connect customers with expert advice at the exact moment of doubt. It works at three touchpoints: on the product detail page (PDP) before purchase, inside the Try at Home portal during a trial, and inside the Returns portal before a return is finalised. You can use Concierge as a standalone product even if you do not offer Try at Home.

## Why styling support matters

Fashion e-commerce faces a costly confidence gap: the average brand loses \$29 per new customer to returns and hesitations, with return rates averaging 30% for first-time buyers. Most shopping decisions happen within 36 hours, which means brands have a narrow window to influence outcomes. Concierge closes that gap by giving customers human, expert reassurance exactly when they need it.

## Where it shows up

<CardGroup>
  <Card title="On the PDP" icon="store">
    A styling call-to-action appears next to the buy button. Shoppers who are unsure about fit, colour, or pairings can book help before they buy.
  </Card>

  <Card title="In Try at Home" icon="package">
    During the trial period, customers access styling guidance inside the Mirra customer portal while they are deciding what to keep or send back.
  </Card>

  <Card title="In Returns" icon="door-open">
    Before a return request is submitted, a styling check-in offers alternatives. Many returns are prevented at this stage.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Availability and pricing

Mirra Concierge is available standalone and can also be combined with Try with Mirra or Mirra Returns. Plans start at \$290 per month.

<Note>
  PDP-only mode is the fastest way to get started and does not require Try at Home or Returns to be active.
</Note>
