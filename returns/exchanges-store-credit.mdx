Most returns platforms default to refund, which means lost revenue for your brand. Mirra Returns flips that model by offering exchange and store credit options first. When a customer is unsure about an item, the right alternative (a different size, colour, or a credit to shop later) often keeps them engaged with your brand instead of sending them to a competitor.

## Exchanges before refund

When a customer initiates a return, Mirra Returns surfaces exchange options as the first step in the decision flow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Show exchange options">
    The return portal presents the customer with available variants (size, colour) for the item they purchased, pulled live from Shopify inventory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reserve the exchange item">
    If a variant is available, Mirra can reserve it temporarily so the customer does not lose it during the return transit period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ship the exchange">
    Once the original item is marked as delivered and inspected, confirm the exchange from the dashboard and dispatch the replacement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Exchanges are processed as new orders in Shopify, which keeps your revenue and inventory records accurate.
</Note>

## Store credit before refund

When the right exchange is not available, Mirra Returns offers store credit next.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Offer store credit">
    At the return decision stage, present the customer with the option to receive store credit for the full order value rather than a refund.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply credit automatically">
    Issue the store credit from the Mirra Returns dashboard. It is typically applied as a Shopify gift card or discount code sent directly to the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drive repurchase">
    Store credit keeps the customer in your ecosystem. Follow up with personalised recommendations or styling guidance to help them use the credit quickly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Mirra Concierge supports retention

Mirra Concierge integrates at the exact moment a customer is deciding whether to keep, exchange, or return. This is a high-intent moment where styling guidance can change the outcome.

- **Styling calls**: Offer a quick video or phone call to help the customer find the right fit or accessorise the piece differently.
- **Styling videos**: Send a short, personalised video demonstrating how to wear or style the item.
- **Styling notes**: Deliver written guidance on fit, layering, or alternative sizes based on the customer's profile.

Concierge can be offered inside the Try at Home portal, the Returns portal, or standalone on the PDP. When a customer is considering a return, a styling session often resolves fit concerns and converts a potential loss into a retained order.
