Mirra Returns is the only returns platform that handles both Try at Home and standard orders in a single, branded portal. Instead of forcing customers through label-only flows, it guides them to a clear decision: keep, exchange, store credit, or refund. That decision-first approach leads to approximately 90% of decisions happening within 36 hours, items back in sellable stock within 6 to 9 days from fulfilment, and up to 75% faster return-to-stock times.

## Why Mirra Returns is different

Most returns tools focus on labels and logistics. Mirra Returns optimises the customer decision at the moment they are most likely to convert.

## How it fits with your Mirra products

Mirra Returns is designed to work alongside the rest of the Mirra platform, or as a standalone returns solution for your brand.

- **Try with Mirra**: Returns are an integral part of the Try at Home loop, since every order is a potential return until the customer confirms keep. Platinum plans include Mirra Returns Growth.
- **Mirra Concierge**: Styling guidance can be offered at the return decision stage, helping customers choose an exchange rather than leaving.
- **Mirra Shop**: Returned items made available again quickly mean better inventory availability for discovery shoppers.
