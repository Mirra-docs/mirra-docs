The Mirra Returns dashboard gives you a central view of every return in progress, whether it comes from a Try at Home order or a standard Shopify purchase. From one screen you can review incoming requests, generate prepaid labels, process customer decisions, and see exactly when items are ready to sell again.

## Return queue statuses

Every return moves through a clear pipeline so you always know what needs your attention.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pending" icon="circle-exclamation">
    The customer has initiated a return and is awaiting your approval or a prepaid label.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Label sent" icon="paper-plane">
    You have generated and sent a return label. The customer can now ship the item back.
  </Card>

  <Card title="In transit" icon="truck-fast">
    The return is on its way back to your warehouse or 3PL and has tracking activity.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Delivered" icon="box">

  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Processing a return

Follow these steps when a return reaches the delivered stage and the customer has submitted their decision.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the returns queue">
    In the Mirra Returns dashboard, filter by **Delivered** status to see items that have arrived and are ready for processing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the customer's decision">
    Open the return record to see whether the customer chose to keep, exchange, receive store credit, or request a refund. For exchanges, confirm the replacement size or colour the customer selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the return">
    Click **Approve return** to confirm you have received the item in acceptable condition and authorise the next action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute the decision">
    Depending on the customer's choice, trigger the appropriate action from the dashboard: issue a refund, create a store credit note, or dispatch an exchange item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move item to inventory">
    Once the item is inspected and approved, mark it as ready for resale so it re-enters your sellable stock and updates your inventory counts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Generating prepaid return labels

Mirra Returns lets you generate prepaid return labels directly within the dashboard.

1. Open the return record.
2. Click **Generate label**.
3. Select your preferred carrier from your connected shipping integrations.
4. Send the label to the customer automatically via email or SMS.

<Note>
  Prepaid labels are available depending on your plan tier and connected carrier integrations. Supported carriers include Australia Post, Royal Mail, ShipStation, Starshipit, and others.
</Note>

## Return reason data as buying signals

Every return in Mirra captures structured reason data: fit, size, colour, style, quality, or other. These fields are collected at the point of decision, when the experience is fresh in the customer's mind.

Use this data to:

- Adjust sizing charts on product pages.
- Update buying and merchandising decisions before the next collection.
- Identify fit issues that styling guidance (Mirra Concierge) could help prevent.
- Reduce repeat returns by addressing root causes.

## Inventory visibility

The dashboard shows the full lifecycle of a returned item:

- In transit to returns facility.
- Delivered and pending inspection.
- Approved and restocked.
- Quarantined or marked for liquidation.

<Warning>
  Do not mark items as restocked before inspecting them. Restocking damaged or incomplete returns can lead to negative downstream reviews and chargebacks.
</Warning>
