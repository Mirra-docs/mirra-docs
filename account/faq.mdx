Here are answers to the questions brands ask most often about getting started with Mirra, how the programmes work, and what to expect.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Mirra Concierge without Try with Mirra?">
    Yes. Mirra Concierge can be used as a standalone styling guidance layer, or combined with Try with Mirra and Mirra Returns. You can offer virtual styling calls, in-boutique appointments, styling videos, and styling notes on your PDP and inside customer portals without using Try at Home.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Mirra Returns for standard orders?">
    Yes. Mirra Returns is a unified returns platform that handles both Try at Home orders and standard orders in a single branded portal. You do not need to run Try with Mirra to use Mirra Returns.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a completed Try with Mirra order?">
    A completed Try with Mirra order is an order that contains at least one Try at Home item. Usage fees are calculated on the final value after the trial period, based only on what the customer decides to keep.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change plans as I grow?">
    Yes. You can upgrade or adjust your plan as your monthly order volume increases. Many brands start on Starter or Pro and move to Platinum as they scale.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you offer custom pricing?">
    Yes. The Enterprise plan is designed for brands with high volumes or specific needs. It includes custom pricing, unlimited orders, a custom implementation, SLA, and executive support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there additional fees?">
    Applicable taxes are added to your subscription and usage fees, depending on your location and billing details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does setup take?">
    Installation is fast through the Shopify App Store. Professional setup and onboarding support is available on the Pro plan and above.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does the payment authorisation work?">
    At checkout, a payment authorisation hold is placed on the customer's card. The customer is only charged for the items they confirm they want to keep after the trial period. If items are returned, the hold is released or adjusted accordingly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if a customer doesn't return items?">
    Mirra's customer portal manages decision deadlines and trial windows. You configure the trial duration, and customers are prompted to confirm keep or return before the deadline. If no decision is made, the brand can configure how the order is resolved according to their policy.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    You can reach the Mirra team at [support@trywithmirra.com](mailto:support@trywithmirra.com). You can also book a demo or call with our team [here](https://meetings-ap1.hubspot.com/lahern/website-meeting-link?).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
