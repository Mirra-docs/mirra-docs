Mirra offers flexible plans designed to grow with your fashion brand. Usage fees are based on completed Try with Mirra orders (orders containing at least one Try at Home item), calculated on the final value after the trial period.

## Try with Mirra plans

| Plan | Monthly fee | Usage fee | Order cap | Key features |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | \$49 | 8% | Up to 50 orders/mo | Standard support |
| Pro | \$670 | 3.5% | Up to 400 orders/mo | Multi-currency, VIP segmentation, priority support |
| Platinum | \$1,680 | 2.5% | Up to 2,000 orders/mo | **Most popular**; includes Mirra Returns Growth, dedicated Success Manager |
| Enterprise | \$3,500 | 1.5% | Unlimited | Custom implementation, SLA, executive support |

### How usage is calculated

A completed Try with Mirra order is an order containing at least one Try at Home item. Usage fees are applied to the final value after the trial period, not the full basket value. If a customer returns items, you only pay on what they keep.

## Mirra Concierge

Add Mirra Concierge for virtual styling, in-boutique appointments, styling videos, and styling notes.

- Available inside the Try at Home portal and the Returns portal.
- Can be used standalone or combined with Try with Mirra.
- Starting at **\$290/mo**.

## Mirra Returns plans

Use Mirra Returns for both Try at Home and standard orders in one unified returns portal.

| Plan | Monthly fee | Included returns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Growth | \$99/mo | Up to 200 returns |
| Pro | \$199/mo | Up to 500 returns |
| Scale | \$349/mo | Up to 1,500 returns |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom volume |

<Note>
  Applicable taxes are added to your subscription and usage fees.
</Note>

## Plan flexibility

You can change plans as your order volume grows, or upgrade to unlock additional features and support tiers. The Platinum plan is the most popular choice among scaling fashion brands because it bundles Mirra Returns Growth with lower usage rates.
