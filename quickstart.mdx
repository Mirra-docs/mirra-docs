This guide walks you through installing Mirra on your Shopify store and launching your first Try at Home experience. By the end, shoppers will see a Try at Home option on your product pages, can place trial orders with no upfront payment, and manage keeps and returns in Mirra's branded customer portal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Mirra from the Shopify App Store">
    Visit the [Mirra app listing](https://apps.shopify.com/try-with-mirra) and click **Add app**. Grant the requested permissions so Mirra can sync products, orders, and customer data with your store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Shopify store">
    Inside the Mirra dashboard, complete the one-click Shopify connection. Mirra will import your catalog and set up the webhooks needed to track Try at Home orders automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your first Try at Home experience">
    Open **Try at Home settings** in the Mirra dashboard and set:

    - **Trial window:** How many days customers have to try items at home (for example, 3, 5, or 7 days).
    - **Item limits:** Maximum number of items allowed per trial order.
    - **Payment authorization:** Whether Mirra places a temporary authorization hold or processes a fully deferred payment.

    These settings control what shoppers see at checkout and in the customer portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Try at Home button to your PDP">
    Mirra injects the Try at Home button directly onto your product detail pages. Verify the button appears on a test product and that the styling matches your theme. You can adjust placement and label copy in the Mirra dashboard if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test a trial order">
    Place a test order from your storefront as a customer. Confirm that:

    1. The checkout shows a \$0 upfront total with an authorization hold.
    2. The order appears in the Mirra dashboard under **Trial orders**.
    3. You receive the branded customer portal link to confirm keep or return.

    If the test order flows through all three stages, your Try at Home experience is live.
  </Step>
</Steps>
