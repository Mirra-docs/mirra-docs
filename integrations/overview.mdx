## Ecommerce

- **Shopify** —Mirra is Shopify-native and installs directly from the App Store. Learn more on the [Shopify integration page](/integrations/shopify).
- **Shopify Plus** —Full support for multi-site and enterprise configurations.

## Marketing and CRM

- **Klaviyo** —Sync Try at Home events for automated post-trial email and SMS flows.
- **Yotpo** —Connect reviews and loyalty with your Try at Home programme.

## Customer Support

- **Gorgias** —Surface Mirra order data, trial status, and return requests inside support tickets.
- **Zendesk** —View Try at Home context alongside standard order information for support agents.

Learn more about [Klaviyo and Gorgias setup](/integrations/klaviyo-gorgias).
