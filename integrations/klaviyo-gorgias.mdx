Mirra works with Klaviyo and Gorgias to keep your marketing and support teams aligned with your Try at Home programme. Sync trial events into Klaviyo for automated flows, or surface Mirra order data inside Gorgias and Zendesk for faster support resolution.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Klaviyo">
    Mirra sends Try at Home events to Klaviyo so you can build automated email and SMS flows.

    ## Supported events

    - **Trial started** —Customer begins a Try at Home order.
    - **Decision made** —Customer confirms what they are keeping or returning.
    - **Item kept** —Customer decides to keep an item after trial.
    - **Return requested** —Customer initiates a return.

    ## Use these events to trigger flows

    - Post-trial thank you and review requests.
    - Nudge campaigns for customers who haven't made a decision within a set timeframe.
    - Win-back flows for partial keepers.
    - SMS reminders before the trial window closes.

    ## How to connect

    1. In Mirra, go to **Integrations \> Klaviyo**.
    2. Authorise your Klaviyo account.
    3. Map Mirra events to your Klaviyo profiles and flows.
    4. Activate your automations.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Gorgias">
    Mirra surfaces Try at Home data inside Gorgias support tickets so agents can help customers without switching tools.

    ## What agents see

    - Trial status (active, decision made, return requested).
    - Items in the Try at Home order.
    - Upcoming decision deadlines.
    - Return labels and fulfilment status.

    ## Benefits

    - Agents resolve questions faster with full context.
    - No manual cross-referencing between Mirra and your support tool.
    - Customers get consistent, informed support.

    ## How to connect

    1. In Mirra, go to **Integrations \> Gorgias**.
    2. Authorise your Gorgias account.
    3. Choose the ticket fields and order data to sync.
    4. Map the integration to your Gorgias workflows.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Zendesk">
    If your team uses Zendesk, Mirra also supports ticket enrichment with Try at Home context.

    - Agents can view trial status and item details inside Zendesk tickets.
    - Setup follows the same authorisation flow as Gorgias.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Combine Klaviyo event data with Gorgias ticket data to create proactive support workflows: for example, trigger a VIP customer alert in Gorgias when a high-value trial is pending.
</Tip>
