Mirra is built natively for Shopify. Whether you are on standard Shopify or Shopify Plus, Mirra installs in minutes and handles order creation, fulfilment, and the customer experience without custom development.

## Install Mirra on Shopify

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Shopify App Store">
    Visit [Mirra on the Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/try-with-mirra).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the app to your store">
    Click **Add app** and approve the requested permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your Try at Home settings">
    Inside the Mirra app, set your trial windows, return policies, and branding for the customer portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the PDP button">
    Enable the Try at Home button on your product detail pages so customers can select items with a \$0 upfront authorisation hold.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review order confirmations">
    Mirra automatically links the customer portal in Shopify order confirmation emails. No extra setup is needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Mirra handles Shopify orders

Mirra creates standard Shopify orders from Try at Home checkouts. This means:

- Your existing fulfilment process is not interrupted.
- Warehouse and shipping integrations continue to work as they do today.
- Inventory is respected in real time.
- Authorisation holds are placed at checkout, and customers are only charged for items they decide to keep.

## Shopify Plus support

For Shopify Plus brands, Mirra supports:

- Multi-site and multi-store configurations.
- Enterprise-level customisation.
- API-first access for complex builds, headless storefronts, or bespoke checkout flows.

<Note>
  Mirra is Shopify-native and API-first. If you are on Shopify Plus or have a custom build, our team can support advanced implementations through our enterprise plan.
</Note>
