A try order is a normal Shopify checkout with a trial on top. Shoppers are not billed for kept items until they finish in the customer portal — or the trial window ends.

## Lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Checkout">
    The shopper adds try-eligible items with the try button and checks out. Mirra records the order as a **try order**. Fulfillment follows your usual Shopify flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivery">
    When the shipment is delivered, the **trial window** starts. Mirra can email or SMS the shopper with the due date and a link to the customer portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    In the portal, the shopper marks each item as **keep**, **return**, or **exchange** (if you offer exchanges). They choose a return method if they are sending items back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return">
    Returned items follow the method you set per country: prepaid label, label in the parcel, shopper-arranged shipping, in-store drop-off, or the shopper’s own tracking. Staff approve, receive, and resolve returns in the **Returns Dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settle">
    Kept items are charged (or the existing card hold is captured). Returned items are refunded, issued as store credit or a gift card, or swapped — based on your [return policies](/merchants/returns).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What shoppers see

The **customer portal** is the shopper UI for decisions, labels, and payment. They should not need Shopify Admin.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="During the trial">
    Order contents, **Trial ends in** countdown, and keep / return / exchange actions per line. Status pills include **Trying**, **Keeping**, **Returning**, and **Exchanging**. A hold may show as **pre-authorised** or **Eligible for Instant Release**. Purchased items sit under **Items that are already yours**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="After they submit">
    Confirmation of what they kept vs returned, return instructions or a label, and any outstanding payment (**Total to Pay**, restocking or return shipping fee).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="If they do nothing">
    Mirra reminds them before the due date. If they still do not complete the order, remaining items are treated as **kept** and charged. Document your store’s exact policy on your own FAQ.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  Trial length is counted from **delivery**, not from checkout. In tests, confirm tracking or use **Click to mark delivered** in the portal so the trial clock can start.
</Warning>

## What merchants see

In Shopify Admin, open the Mirra app:

| Screen | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Try Orders** | Review open try orders, copy the portal URL, capture payment, and manage trial lines |
| **Settings** | Trial days, products, fees, countries, and return methods |
| **Returns Dashboard** | Approve, receive, and pay out returns |
| **Analytics** | Try vs buy performance, return reasons, and kept products |

On a Shopify order, the **Mirra Returns** block shows return status. Pin it so your team always sees it.

See the [customer portal](/shoppers/customer-portal) page for shopper-facing wording you can reuse in brand emails.
