After you mark items **return** in the [customer portal](/shoppers/customer-portal), you choose how they go back. The store decides which options you see.

## Choose a return method

On the return step you may see:

| Option | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Download label now** | Get a prepaid label in the portal |
| **Email return instructions** | Wait for a label by email |
| **Use shipping label provided by store** | Use the label that came in the original parcel |
| **Self return** | Buy postage and send to the **Return address** |
| **Drop off at a store** | Take items to a listed location |
| **I have my own return label** | Enter your tracking number and carrier |

Refund choice, if offered: **How would you like your refund?** — **Original payment method**, **Store credit**, or **Gift card**.

## After you submit

Success copy is **Payment successful**, **Order complete**, or **Thank you for confirming your return**. Then:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the label or address">
    **Download return label** opens **Print your shipping label** → **Repack your items** → **Post your return**. A toast may say **We've emailed you your label**.

    Self-ship shows a copyable **Return address**. In-store shows the drop-off address and **Keep your receipt**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post within the stated time">
    Many stores ask you to post within **3 days** after you complete the order. Follow the instructions on screen if they differ.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track the return">
    Stages can include: Return submitted → Label ready → In transit → Received → Reviewed → Refunded (or hold released). Use **Resend email** if you need the label again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the label is not ready yet, you may see **Getting your return label ready…** or a waiting message while the store uploads your label (often 1–2 business days).

<Warning>
  If a return is overdue, send the items back so you are not charged for them. Contact the store if you cannot post in time.
</Warning>

## Need help?

Ask the store you ordered from about return windows, fees, and refund timing. Portal login issues: [support@trywithmirra.com](mailto:support@trywithmirra.com).
