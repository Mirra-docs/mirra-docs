After you mark items **return** in the [customer portal](/shoppers/customer-portal), you choose how they go back. The store decides which options you see.

For purchased items, the timeline can show **Return window active** — for example **Your order is complete and still returnable within N days from delivery** (or **from purchase** / **from fulfilment**). Mixed carts may say **Purchased items are still returnable within N days from delivery** or **Purchased items follow the store's return policy. Trial items follow your try period.** Open **How does the return window work?** or the item **Return window** note if you need the dates.

## Choose a return method

On the return step you may see:

| Option | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Download prepaid label** | Get a prepaid label in the portal. If the fee is waived this is the free option |
| **Deduct the return label fee from your refund** | The store generates a label and takes the fee from a purchased-item refund |
| **Purchase a return label** | The store generates a label. The fee is charged when you complete the return |
| **Email return instructions** | Wait for a label by email |
| **Use shipping label provided by store** | Use the label that came in the original parcel |
| **Self return** | Buy postage and send to the **Return address** |
| **Drop off at a store** | Take items to a listed location |
| **I have my own return label** | Enter your tracking number and carrier |

Refund choice, if offered: **How would you like your refund?** — **Original payment method**, **Store credit**, or **Gift card**. If you paid the original order with store credit or a gift card, those payout options may not appear.

If a return fee is waived, the option and payment summary can show **Free** instead of an amount.

If you return a gift line, confirm keep or return in the **Gift Item** dialog first.

## After you submit

Success copy is **Payment successful**, **Order complete**, or **Thank you for confirming your return**. Then:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the label or address">
    If the store auto-approves your return, you may go straight to **Getting your return label ready** — you can leave the page; they notify you when the label is ready.

    If they review first, you will see **Return request submitted** and **We're reviewing your request**.

    **Download return label** opens **Print your shipping label** → **Repack your items** → **Post your return**. A toast may say **We've emailed you your label**.

    Self-ship shows a copyable **Return address**. In-store shows the drop-off address and **Keep your receipt**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post within the stated time">
    Many stores ask you to post within **3 days** after you complete the order. Follow the instructions on screen if they differ.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track the return">
    Stages can include: Return submitted → Label ready → In transit → Received → Reviewed → Refunded (or hold released). Use **Resend email** if you need the label again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the label is not ready yet, you may see **Getting your return label ready…** or a waiting message while the store uploads your label (often 1–2 business days).

If a US label cannot be created, **Mailing address needed** asks you to enter **Street or PO Box**, optional **Apartment, suite, unit**, **City**, **State**, and **ZIP**. A PO Box is fine. Click **Save and retry label**, or **Go to dashboard**. If it still fails: **USPS still could not accept that mailing address.**

For some Australian labels you may see **Address Verification Required** — pick the matching suburb and click **Update Suburb**.

If you chose store credit or a gift card, wait for **How to use your store credit** or **How to use your gift card**. Until then: **Your store credit isn't ready yet** / **Your gift card isn't ready yet**. Visit the brand’s site, log in to your customer account for credit, or enter the gift card code at checkout.

<Warning>
  If a return is overdue, send the items back so you are not charged for them. Contact the store if you cannot post in time.
</Warning>
