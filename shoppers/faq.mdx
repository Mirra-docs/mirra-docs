Answers if you placed a try order. Brand-specific questions (trial length, fees, exchanges) go to the **store you ordered from**.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When does the trial window start?">
    The trial starts when the order is **delivered**, not when you check out. Open the [customer portal](/shoppers/customer-portal) and look for **Trial ends in**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I do nothing?">
    The store reminds you before the due date. If you still do not complete the order, remaining items are treated as **kept** and charged.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When am I charged?">
    You pay for kept items when you finish in the portal, or when the trial ends. Checkout may show a hold, a deposit, or \$0 — depending on the store.

    Amounts marked **Eligible for Instant Release** are on hold. Return the item and the hold is released straight away. A deposit is refunded for items you return; kept items are charged for the remaining balance.

    A **Pending** or **Processing** line on your card is the hold, not a completed payment. Banks often keep it for 5–7 business days. The store cannot remove it — contact your bank if it stays longer. A second pending line is usually a refreshed hold, not a second charge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Afterpay, a gift card, or a voucher at checkout?">
    No. A try order needs a saved card. PayPal, Afterpay, and other pay-later options are not available. You can apply a gift card or voucher later in the [customer portal](/shoppers/customer-portal) when you pay for kept items.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When is shipping charged?">
    If every line is try, shipping is usually charged when you finish — with kept items. If you also buy something, add a gift, or add shipping protection, shipping is often due at checkout. You still pay shipping if you return everything. Click & Collect does not remove that outbound shipping charge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get more time?">
    In the last 24 hours you may get a text and see **Request time extension** in the [customer portal](/shoppers/customer-portal). That adds 48 hours. If the option is not there yet, check again closer to the due date, or contact the store.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I use store credit or a gift card?">
    After the store issues it, the portal shows **How to use your store credit** or **How to use your gift card**. Credit applies at checkout when you are logged in to that brand’s customer account. Gift cards have a code to enter at checkout. Until then you see **Your store credit isn't ready yet** (or the gift card equivalent).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I open the customer portal from Shopify?">
    On the Shopify order status page you may see **Open Mirra Customer Portal**. You also get the same link in delivery and reminder messages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Contact the **brand** you ordered from first (the store’s customer care). For portal login issues, email [support@trywithmirra.com](mailto:support@trywithmirra.com).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
