The customer portal is where you manage a try order after it arrives. You get there from an email or text link, or by signing in with the email or mobile number you used at checkout.

This page is shopper-facing language brands can reuse in emails and FAQs.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your link">
    Use **Manage Your Order**, **Account Login**, or the link in your delivery or reminder message. A one-click link can take you straight in. Some stores also show **Open Mirra Customer Portal** on the Shopify order status page in your customer account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or request a code">
    On **Login to manage your order**, enter your **email or mobile number** and click **Continue**. Enter the 6-digit **one-time password**. Use **Use a different email or phone** if you need to switch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  There is no order-number lookup. Use the same email or phone as the order. If the code fails, you will see **Invalid one-time password! Please try again.** Login help: [support@trywithmirra.com](mailto:support@trywithmirra.com).
</Info>

After login you see **Your active orders** and **Order history**. Open an order to continue.

## After your order arrives

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the trial countdown">
    The order page shows **Trial ends in** and the due date. Tap the info icon for **How does the trial work?** — a hold is not a charge; a deposit is refunded for items you return.

    Amounts marked **Eligible for Instant Release** are on hold. Return the item and the hold is released straight away. If you keep it, the hold becomes a charge when the trial ends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark delivered if you need to">
    If the parcel is in your hands but the trial has not started, use **Click to mark delivered**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose keep, return, or exchange">
    Click **Complete order** or **Return or exchange items**. Everything starts in **Keep**. Use **Return instead** or **Return or exchange** to move a line. Use **Keep instead** to move it back.

    Footer counts: **Keeping N · Returning N**. If you return everything, you may see **You aren't keeping any items. Please note this will incur a fee.**

    Gift lines open a **Gift Item** dialog — choose keep or return. Purchased (non-try) items sit under **Items that are already yours** and follow the store’s purchased return window, not the trial. If Mirra does not manage those returns, use the store’s message or **Start a return**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a return reason">
    For returns, choose a reason (too small, too big, damaged, not your style, and similar). Some stores ask for photos. You can **Exchange this item instead** if the store offers exchanges.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and pay">
    **Review your order** shows **Total to Pay**, anything already paid, and any **Restocking Fee**, **Return Shipping Fee**, or **Return Label Fee** (the store chooses the label). A waived fee can show as **Free**. Pay with **Pay with card** or **Pay with alternative method**. If the store charges when the return is received, you may **Confirm hold adjustment** instead of paying now.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Status pills on items: **Trying**, **Keeping**, **Purchased**, **Returning**, **Returned**, **Exchanging**, **Gift**.

The order page can show a **pre-authorised** amount. Tap it for **Authorisation history**. If a hold could not be placed, use **Authorize**. Without that, the order can be delayed or cancelled.

If the order arrives in more than one package, you may see **Order partially delivered** or **Your order is being delivered in N packages**. Your trial starts once all items have been delivered.

If something did not finish, look for **Action needed on your order**. Follow **Start return or complete order**, **Retry completion**, **Retry payment**, or **Open invoice to pay**.

<Info>
  If you do nothing before the trial ends, the store may charge you for remaining items. Check **Trial ends in** on the order. Some stores offer **Request time extension** on the last day.
</Info>

## Exchanges

If the store offers exchanges, pick a replacement (**Choose replacement** / **Change replacement**). A notice card shows the deadline first — for example **Complete your new order by** a date **to skip** the restocking or return shipping fee. Expand it for numbered steps. Collapsed, you still see the item summary and a **Due** pill.

You then **Continue to choose your items** on the storefront and check out with the **same email or phone number** before the exchange window ends.

You may see **You're ready to select your exchange items** and a countdown. Some stores keep the picker in the portal: **Submit your return**, then **Checkout in a new tab** or **Copy checkout link**, then wait for confirmation. After checkout, **Pay and complete exchange** or **Submit and go to checkout**. Use **Edit exchange items** if you need to change picks.

On **Review your order**, if a fee is waived until the deadline, tick the confirmation (**I'll complete my exchange order by then…**) before you continue. Open **What's this?** or **How this works** if you need the steps again.

If the store reviews the return first, you may see **Your return is being reviewed** — your exchange picks are saved. After they accept it, **Waiting for your store credit** (or gift card) appears until they issue it. Then you can **Start your exchange**.

If the window ends you will see **Exchange window expired**. Contact the store if you still need a swap.

## Store credit and gift cards

If you chose **Store credit**, the portal shows **How to use your store credit** after the store issues it: visit the brand’s site, log in to your **customer account**, and apply credit at checkout.

If you chose **Gift card**, **How to use your gift card** shows a code you can copy. Add items to your cart and enter the code at checkout. Status can be **Active**, **Fully used**, or **Expired**.

Until then you may see **Your store credit isn't ready yet** or **Your gift card isn't ready yet**. The store emails you when it is ready.

## Styling sessions

After delivery, **Book a styling session** can appear on the order. Choose **Video call**, **Phone call**, or **In-store**. If the store offers more than one session in a channel, pick **Choose an appointment type**. For in-store, **Choose a store**, then pick a time.

Reschedule or cancel if the store allows it. You may be asked for a photo. Some stores also show **Films** and **Tips**. If you already have a booking for that order, the portal says so.

## If payment fails

You may get **Payment unsuccessful** or an email to **Retry payment** / **Make Payment**. Open the invoice if the portal asks you to. Card messages include insufficient funds, expired card, or invalid details.

If a hold could not be placed, you may see **Payment authorization required** — tap **Authorize**. Without that, the order can be delayed or cancelled.

## Need help?

On the order, click **Get help with order**. Contact the **brand** you ordered from first (the store’s customer care). For portal login issues, email [support@trywithmirra.com](mailto:support@trywithmirra.com).
