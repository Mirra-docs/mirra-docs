You can try items at home before you pay for what you keep. Look for a try button on the product page — often **Try Before You Buy**, **Home Try-On**, or **Try at Home**.

## Add items to try

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a try-eligible product">
    Choose size or colour as usual. Click the try button. An info icon or **TRY WITH MIRRA** mark also opens the how-it-works steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read how it works">
    The **TRY BEFORE YOU BUY** modal covers:

    1. **Select Try Before You Buy**
    2. Payment — a hold, a refundable deposit, or saved card details. You are not charged for kept items until you decide (or the trial ends)
    3. **Try at Home** — you have a set number of days after the order **arrives**
    4. **Easy Returns** — pay for what you keep and return the rest. A restocking or return shipping fee may apply

    Click **Continue**. By continuing you agree to the store’s **Terms and Conditions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your cart">
    You may see **Try Before You Buy all eligible items**. Turn that on to set every eligible line to a home trial. Some items in the same cart can still be a normal purchase.

    If the cart says you have too many try items, remove extras until you are within the store’s limit. Some stores add the extra item as a **normal purchase** instead — you may see **Max Try items reached. Item was added for purchase.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check out">
    Complete Shopify checkout. You may need to tick that you understand your card will be authorised for the total shown. Add a phone number if the checkout asks for one — it is used to reach your order later.

    Try items often show **$0 at checkout** or a hold, depending on the store.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After you order

You get an order confirmation. When the parcel is on its way or delivered, the store emails or texts a link to the **customer portal**. Open that link to keep, return, or exchange.

Some stores text you at delivery. If the message says you can keep everything by replying **YES**, that completes the order with every item kept.

If the store offers styling help, you may also see **Book a styling session** on the product page (often **Free** plus a session length). Choose **Video call**, **Phone call**, or **In-store**. If there is more than one option in a channel, pick **Choose an appointment type**. For in-store, **Choose a store** first, then pick a time and **Book appointment**. After delivery you can book, reschedule, or cancel from the [customer portal](/shoppers/customer-portal) as well.

<Info>
  Your trial starts when the order is **delivered**, not when you check out. If only some items have arrived, the portal may wait until the rest are delivered.
</Info>
