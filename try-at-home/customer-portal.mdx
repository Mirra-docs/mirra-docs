sidebarTitle: "Customer Portal"

---

After a Try at Home order is delivered, shoppers are invited to the branded customer portal to review their trial and confirm what they want to keep or send back. The portal is match-matched to your brand and replaces manual emails or support tickets with a clean, self-service experience.

## What the portal shows

When a shopper opens their portal, they see:

- The items currently in their trial
- Photos and names of each product
- Buttons to choose **Keep**, **Return**, or **Exchange** per item
- The current status of their trial and how much time is left in the decision window
- A clear breakdown of what they will be charged if they confirm their choices

## How shoppers access the portal

Access is sent automatically at delivery via email and SMS. The link is unique to the order and does not require a password or login. Shoppers can revisit the portal at any time until the trial window closes.

## Timeline and charges

Most shoppers decide quickly. Around 90% of keep and return decisions are made within 36 hours of delivery. Once the shopper confirms their choices, Mirra finalises payment and charges them only for the items they keep. Any returns are shipped with a pre-generated label created inside the portal.

## Concierge inside the portal

If you have Mirra Concierge enabled, styling features appear directly inside the customer portal while the shopper is deciding. Shoppers can book a styling call, view styling notes, or watch a styling video without leaving the page. This creates a seamless moment of support exactly when the shopper is evaluating fit and feel.

<Tip>
  Learn how to enable styling calls, notes, and video in the portal on the [Concierge Overview](/concierge/overview).
</Tip>

## Returns and exchanges

Returned items from the portal are routed through Mirra Returns. If you are on a plan that includes Returns, the Return and Exchange options are visible automatically. Store credit can also be offered as an alternative to a refund depending on your returns settings.
