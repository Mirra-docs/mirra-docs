Try with Mirra lets your customers try items at home before they pay. On your Shopify product pages, shoppers choose Try at Home and checkout with \$0 upfront, paying only a pre-authorisation hold. They receive the items, try them on, and confirm what to keep or return through a branded portal. You ship orders through your standard workflow, so nothing changes behind the scenes.

This page covers why Try at Home matters and the results brands see when they add Mirra to their checkout.

## Why Try at Home converts

Fashion has one of the highest cart abandonment rates online. Between 75% and 82% of fashion carts are abandoned, and sizing uncertainty is a major cause. Try at Home removes that risk for the shopper, which directly improves your conversion rates and reduces reliance on markdowns and promotions.

## How Try at Home works at a glance

- Shoppers select **Try at Home** directly on the product page
- They checkout with **\$0 upfront** and a pre-authorisation hold only
- Items ship through your normal Shopify fulfilment process
- The shopper receives a branded decision portal to confirm what they keep or return
- You are only charged for what they keep, and returns are recovered fast
