Try at Home connects your Shopify product pages to a branded customer portal without changing how you ship orders. Shoppers see a Try at Home option on the product page, check out with \$0 upfront, and manage their trial through a portal you control. For your brand, the order is a standard Shopify order until the shopper confirms what they keep.

## Shopper journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Try at Home on the product page">
    On your product display page, shoppers choose the Try at Home option. They add items to cart and proceed to checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Checkout with $0 upfront">
    At checkout, shoppers place a pre-authorisation hold on their card. No money is taken. The order is created in Shopify and ships as normal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive and try items at home">
    Items arrive through your standard fulfilment process. The shopper tries everything on at home, with no rush and no upfront commitment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide via the branded portal">
    Within the trial window, the shopper opens your branded customer portal to confirm which items they want to keep, return, or exchange.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Charged only for kept items">
    Mirra finalises the charge for the kept items. Anything returned ships back with a pre-generated return label. Inventory is recovered fast.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  ~90% of shoppers make their keep or return decision within 36 hours of receiving their items.
</Note>

## What changes for your brand

Almost nothing, by design. The order lifecycle within Shopify remains the same as a standard order:

- Orders still appear in your Shopify admin and can follow your normal fulfilment workflow
- Inventory deducts when the order is placed and restores when returns are confirmed
- You do not need to manage separate shipping rules or warehouse processes for Try at Home orders

Mirra sits between the customer decision and the final charge, so your operations team works with familiar tools while the shopper gets a modern, flexible experience.

## Where returns fit in

All Try at Home returns are managed alongside standard returns inside Mirra Returns. If a shopper returns part of their trial, the return label is generated automatically and the item is tracked through the same portal. You can learn more about the unified returns flow on the Returns pages.
