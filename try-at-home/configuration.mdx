You control how Try at Home appears on your store, who sees it, and what they can try. Configuration is managed inside the Mirra dashboard and applied directly to your Shopify storefront. Use the settings below to match the experience to your brand and protect your margins.

## Core settings

The following settings can be adjusted from the Try at Home configuration panel.

| Setting | What it controls | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trial window | How long shoppers have to try items before confirming a decision | 36 hours, 72 hours, custom |
| Item limit per trial | Maximum items a shopper can include in a single Try at Home order | Up to 5 items |
| Eligible products | Which products or collections support Try at Home | New arrivals, full-price only |
| Country targeting | Markets where Try at Home is available | Australia, UK, US |
| Customer segment targeting | Which shoppers see the option | VIP, loyalty members, geographic regions |
| Silent discounting | Internal margin protection applied behind the scenes | Automatically adjusts pricing logic |
| Brand look and feel | Colours, fonts, and language matching your store | Brand-matched PDP badge and portal |
| Discount and care rules | Allowed promotions, return wear-and-tear policies | Exclude discount codes, set try-and-wear terms |

## Recommended rollout strategy

Use a controlled launch to protect your operations and validate demand.

1. **Segment**: Start with a small, high-trust customer group such as VIPs or loyalty members in a single country.
2. **Validate**: Monitor conversion lift, return rates, and fulfilment impact for two to four weeks.
3. **Scale**: Expand to new collections, customer segments, or regions based on results.

<Note>
  Advanced controls including detailed segment targeting, custom trial windows, and discount rule management are available on Pro plans and above.
</Note>
